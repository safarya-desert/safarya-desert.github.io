Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We collect the minimum data needed to run your booking. This policy explains what we collect, why, for how long, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Booking details: name, phone/WhatsApp number, journey, date, number and age groups of travellers, accommodation choice, and any notes you write.
  • Conversations: if you use the booking assistant on this site or message us on WhatsApp, we keep the conversation so we can continue helping you and review service quality.
  • Limited technical data: your IP address is used briefly to prevent abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored with your booking.

We never ask for card details on this website. Your passport is only needed on the day of the trip for police checkpoints.

How we use it

  • To record and manage your booking and contact you about it (confirmation, deposit details, changes).
  • To answer your questions in the chat or on WhatsApp.
  • We do not send marketing messages without your explicit consent, and we never sell your data.

The AI booking assistant

The assistant uses an AI model from Google (Gemini) to understand and answer your messages, so the text of your messages is sent to Google for processing. It can only reach our database through a fixed set of functions (searching journeys, availability, price, creating a booking after you agree, checking or cancelling a booking with its reference and your phone number). Please don't share sensitive data such as card numbers in the chat.

Service providers that process data for us

  • Cloudflare — hosting of the booking server and database.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — if you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Google (Gemini API) — processing booking-assistant messages.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the website pages.
  • Wikimedia — images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons servers.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations are deleted automatically 90 days after the last message.
  • Booking records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion (within what the law requires us to keep), or object to its use, in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. Contact us through the contact page.

Security

Connections to the website and server are encrypted (HTTPS). Customer data is only accessible to the business owner through a password-protected admin panel, and every change to a booking is logged.

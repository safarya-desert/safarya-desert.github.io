Terms & cancellation policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Booking

  • Booking on the website, in the chat or on WhatsApp creates a provisional booking with a reference (like SD-AB12CD), and your places are held for 48 hours.
  • The booking becomes confirmed once we receive the deposit (30% of the total). If the hold expires without a deposit, the places are released.
  • Book at least 3 days before the trip (6 weeks for the Gilf Kebir, because of security permits).
  • The Gilf Kebir expedition is priced on request by group size; a request is not a booking until you accept our quote.

2. Prices and payment

  • Prices are per person: in US dollars for international travellers and in Egyptian pounds for Egyptian residents (park fees for foreign visitors are higher).
  • A private trip adds 45% and uses a whole vehicle (4 seats). The premium accommodation upgrade adds 15%.
  • Children under 2 travel free (without a separate seat), ages 2–5 pay 25%, ages 6–11 pay 50%, and from 12 the adult price.
  • The deposit can be paid by card, bank transfer, InstaPay or Vodafone Cash as available; the balance is paid on the day. Nothing is charged on this website.

3. Cancellation and changes

  • 7 days or more before the trip: full refund of the deposit.
  • 3 to 6 days before: 50% refund.
  • Less than 3 days: no refund, but you can move your date within 6 months.
  • If we cancel because of weather or permits: a full refund or a new date, as you prefer.
  • You can cancel on the Manage booking page or by contacting us.

4. Safety

  • Vehicles travel in convoys of at least two, with a Thuraya satellite phone and a first-aid kit.
  • Please follow the instructions of your guide and driver; we may adjust the programme if weather or security require it.
  • Carry your passport for police checkpoints.

5. Environment

Protected areas are protected by law: taking rocks, fossils or Libyan Desert Glass is forbidden, and nothing may be left behind.

6. Photos

Location photos on this website are from Wikimedia Commons under free licences (see photo credits). They illustrate the places and are not photos of our own trips.

Protected areas

The landscapes we travel through

Park entry is included, and our guides explain the geology and history of every place.

© Asgalal · CC BY-SA 4.0
Protected since 2002

White Desert National Park

Chalk formations carved by the wind into mushrooms, chickens and camels, north of Farafra Oasis.

© AhmedMosaad · CC BY-SA 4.0
UNESCO World Heritage, 2005

Wadi Al-Hitan (Whale Valley)

Skeletons of 40-million-year-old whales that still had hind legs, in the heart of the Fayoum desert.

© Ibrahim El-Mezayen · CC BY-SA 4.0
Protected since 1989

Wadi El Rayan Protected Area

Two lakes joined by Egypt's only waterfalls, with dunes, springs and migrating birds.

© Marsupium · CC BY 4.0
Protected since 2002

Siwa Protected Area

Salt lakes and hundreds of natural springs, white gazelle and fennec fox, within a unique Amazigh culture.

© Roland Unger · CC BY-SA 3.0
National park since 2007

Gilf Kebir National Park

A plateau in the far south-west with rock art thousands of years old, including the Cave of Swimmers.

© Ahmedherz · CC BY-SA 4.0
On the Bahariya route

Black Desert & Crystal Mountain

Volcanic hills capped with black stone, and a ridge of quartz crystal on the road to Farafra.

Everything the journey needs

What's included

Transfers

Air-conditioned minibus from Cairo and Giza, then a Land Cruiser 4x4 in the desert.

Accommodation

Salt-and-mud eco-lodges in Siwa, a hotel in Bahariya, a Bedouin camp in the White Desert.

Meals

Breakfast, lunch and a Bedouin charcoal-grilled dinner, with water, tea and coffee throughout.

Park fees

White Desert, Wadi El Rayan and Whale Valley tickets, and Great Sand Sea permits.

Safari guide

An English-speaking guide, plus a Bedouin guide from the region.

Experiences

Sandboarding, swimming in sulphur springs, stargazing and sunrise photography.

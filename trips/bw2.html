White & Black Desert Overnight

Cairo → Bahariya → Black Desert → Crystal Mountain → White Desert

© Vyacheslav Argenberg · CC BY 4.0

White Desert

Egypt's classic desert journey: the volcanic Black Desert, Crystal Mountain, and a night under the stars among the chalk sculptures of the White Desert.

2 days / 1 nightLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Day 1

6 am departure to Bahariya (4 hours) and lunch, then by Land Cruiser to the Black Desert, Crystal Mountain and Agabat Valley. Sunset among the White Desert formations, barbecue dinner and camp.

Day 2

Sunrise at the Mushroom and Chicken rocks, breakfast, the Old White Desert, a hot spring in Bahariya, and back in Cairo around 6 pm.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Bedouin camp
  • 4 meals
  • Hot spring
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

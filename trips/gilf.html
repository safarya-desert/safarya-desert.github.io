Gilf Kebir Expedition

Dakhla → Great Sand Sea → Gilf Kebir → Cave of Swimmers

© Roland Unger · CC BY-SA 3.0

Gilf Kebir Plateau

Twelve days in Egypt's far south-west: the Libyan Desert Glass field, the Gilf Kebir plateau and the Cave of Swimmers. Priced on request by group size.

12 daysLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / GizaPriced on request

Itinerary

Days 1–2

Cairo to Dakhla, convoy preparation and military escort.

Days 3–5

Crossing the Great Sand Sea and the Libyan Desert Glass field.

Days 6–9

The Gilf Kebir plateau: Cave of Swimmers, Cave of Beasts and Wadi Sura.

Days 10–12

Return across the desert to Dakhla and Cairo.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Security permits
  • 3-vehicle convoy
  • Cook & full camp
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

Places on this journey

Gilf Kebir Plateau · Cave of Swimmers, Wadi Sura · Great Sand Sea · Libyan Desert Glass

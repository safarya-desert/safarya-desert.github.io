The Great Oases Loop: Bahariya to Kharga

Bahariya → Farafra → Dakhla (Al-Qasr) → Kharga

© Vyacheslav Argenberg · CC BY 2.0

Al-Qasr, Dakhla Oasis

A week along the four-oasis road: the White Desert, the medieval town of Al-Qasr, Deir el-Hagar, the Temple of Hibis and the Bagawat necropolis.

7 days / 6 nightsLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Days 1–2

Bahariya and the White Desert.

Day 3

Farafra and Bir Sitta.

Days 4–5

Dakhla: the Islamic town of Al-Qasr, the Muzawaka tombs and Deir el-Hagar.

Day 6

Kharga: the Temple of Hibis and the Bagawat tombs.

Day 7

Return to Cairo or Luxor.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Hotels + camps
  • All meals
  • Archaeology guide
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

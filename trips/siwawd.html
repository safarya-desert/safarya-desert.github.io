Siwa to the White Desert

Cairo → Siwa → Desert track → Bahariya → White Desert

© Sara Nabih · CC BY-SA 4.0

Fatnas Island, Siwa

The remote 400 km desert track from Siwa to Bahariya on a special permit, ending with a night in the White Desert.

5 days / 4 nightsLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Days 1–2

Siwa: the lakes, Shali and the Great Sand Sea.

Day 3

The desert track from Siwa to Bahariya (400 km) with stops at remote springs.

Day 4

Black and White Deserts, a night under the stars.

Day 5

Return to Cairo.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Lodge + camps
  • All meals
  • Siwa–Bahariya permit
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

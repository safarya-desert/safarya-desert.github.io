Bahariya & the White Desert, 3 Days

Cairo → Bahariya → Black Desert → White Desert → El Hayz

© Ahmedherz · CC BY-SA 4.0

Black Desert

A slower pace: one night in a Bahariya hotel and one in a desert camp, with El Hayz village and the oasis springs.

3 days / 2 nightsLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Bahariya, tour the oasis, the museum and the Bishmu spring, overnight in a hotel.

Day 2

Black Desert, Crystal Mountain, El Hayz village, camp in the New White Desert.

Day 3

The Old White Desert at sunrise, a hot spring, return to Cairo.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Hotel + camp
  • All meals
  • El Hayz village
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

Places on this journey

Black Desert · Bahariya Oasis (Gebel el-Dist) · Crystal Mountain · White Desert rock formations · White Desert

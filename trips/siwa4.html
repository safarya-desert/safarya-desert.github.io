Complete Siwa + a Night in the Great Sand Sea

Cairo → Siwa → Great Sand Sea → Bir Wahed

© Roland Unger · CC BY-SA 3.0

Great Sand Sea

All of Siwa at an easy pace, plus a night in a Bedouin camp among the dunes of the Great Sand Sea.

4 days / 3 nightsLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Siwa, check in to a kershef (salt-mud) eco-lodge, sunset at Fatnas.

Day 2

Salt lakes, the Oracle Temple, Cleopatra's Spring and Gebel Dakrour.

Day 3

Full-day safari in the Great Sand Sea, sandboarding, the Bir Wahed hot spring and a Bedouin camp.

Day 4

Shali fortress and the old market, return to Cairo.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • Lodge + camp
  • All meals
  • Siwan evening
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

Places on this journey

Great Sand Sea · Shali fortress, Siwa · Fatnas Island, Siwa · Siwa salt pools · Temple of the Oracle of Amun, Siwa

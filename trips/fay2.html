Fayoum Overnight: Stargazing Camp in the Reserve

Cairo → Wadi El Rayan → Star camp → Tunis village

© Ibrahim El-Mezayen · CC BY-SA 4.0

Gebel el-Madwara, Wadi El Rayan

A night in a desert camp inside the reserve with telescope stargazing, then sunrise over the dunes. Our only departure that runs through the summer.

2 days / 1 nightLand Cruiser 4x44 per vehiclePick-up in Cairo / Giza

Itinerary

Day 1

Wadi El Rayan and the waterfalls, sunset from the dunes, barbecue dinner and a telescope stargazing session.

Day 2

Sunrise, breakfast at camp, Wadi Al-Hitan, Tunis pottery village, return to Cairo.

Included

  • Transfers from Cairo and Giza in an air-conditioned vehicle
  • Land Cruiser 4x4 with a desert driver
  • 3 meals
  • Camp
  • Telescope
  • Park entry fees
  • Safari guide
  • Water, tea and coffee

Not included

  • Soft drinks and snacks
  • Tips
  • Optional activities such as camel rides or quad bikes

Places on this journey

Gebel el-Madwara, Wadi El Rayan · Wadi El Rayan lake · Wadi Al-Hitan (Whale Valley) · Tunis village, Fayoum

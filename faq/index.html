Before you travel

Frequently asked questions

Where and when do trips start?

We pick you up from your hotel in Cairo or Giza (or meet at Tahrir Square, Giza or 6th of October). Departures are early, between 4 and 6 am, so we reach Bahariya or Siwa before noon.

Do I need a visa or special permits?

You need a valid Egyptian tourist visa and your passport on you — there are police checkpoints on desert roads. We arrange all park entry tickets and desert permits; the Gilf Kebir and the Siwa–Bahariya track need security permits that we apply for in advance.

What should I pack?

A warm jacket for the night (winter nights drop to around 5°C), sunglasses, sunscreen, swimwear for the springs and lakes, closed shoes, and your passport.

How much do children pay?

Under 2 free, ages 2–5 pay 25%, ages 6–11 pay 50%, and from 12 the full adult price.

What is the camp like?

Two-person dome tents with mattresses, blankets and sleeping bags, a large Bedouin tent for meals and the evening, and a portable chemical toilet. The premium upgrade gives you a larger tent with a proper bed and lighting.

How is my booking confirmed?

Submitting the form creates a provisional booking with a reference number, and your places are held for 48 hours. We then send deposit details (30%), and the booking becomes confirmed once it arrives. You can check the status any time on the Manage booking page with your reference and phone number.

How do I pay?

The 30% deposit can be paid by card, bank transfer, InstaPay or Vodafone Cash, and the balance on the day of the trip. Nothing is charged on this website.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 7 days before the trip, 50% between 3 and 6 days, and under 3 days you can move your date within 6 months. If we cancel because of weather or permits, you get a full refund.

Is it safe?

Every vehicle travels with at least one other car, carries a Thuraya satellite phone and a first-aid kit, and is driven by licensed drivers from the oases who know the routes without GPS.

Do you run trips in summer?

June to August is very hot, so only the Fayoum overnight stargazing trip runs. Other trips run from September/October to April/May depending on the route.

What does the Gilf Kebir expedition require?

A security permit issued 4–6 weeks in advance, an official escort and a convoy of at least 3 vehicles. That's why it is priced per group — send us a request and we'll reply with a quote.
